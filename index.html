Great Burgers, Even Better Vibes Casual enough for a quick bite but cozy enough to linger and enjoy your meal. I ordered their signature burger, and it did not disappoint. The smashed patty was juicy and seasoned perfectly, with a satisfying char that added just the right amount of smokiness. The bun was soft but sturdy (no falling apart halfway through!), and the toppings were fresh and generously portioned. The fries were crisp, golden, and addictive, especially with the red dragon sauce. It is spicy though.!! Overall, this place nails the fundamentals: great food, quick service, and a welcoming vibe. If you’re craving a well-crafted burger, this spot is definitely worth the visit.
